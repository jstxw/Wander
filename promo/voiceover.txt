The web can feel like a maze. Menus inside menus. Unfamiliar words. And too many places to click.

Meet Wander: a calm guide that helps you learn any ordinary website by doing.

Just ask what you want to accomplish. Type your question, tap to talk, or simply say, “Hello Wander.”

Wander studies the page and gives you one clear next step. A yellow marker shows exactly where to go, while you stay in control of every click, every word, and every decision.

Take a wrong turn? No problem. Wander looks again and adapts to where you are. Pause when you need a break, resume when you’re ready, or stop at any time.

Behind the scenes, Wander can inspect a public page in a separate practice browser, rehearse safe navigation, and save evidence for the guidance it gives you. Your login, passwords, payments, and final sensitive actions always stay with you.

From government services and online shopping, to travel, job applications, and everyday tasks, Wander helps make the web feel possible.

Wander. Wherever you're headed, one step at a time.

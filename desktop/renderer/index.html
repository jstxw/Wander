Allow Accessibility access

Wander reads the buttons and menus of the app you choose so it can highlight the next one. It never clicks or types for you.

  1. Click Open System Settings.
  2. Turn on Wander under Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  3. Come back and click Check again.

Guide this Mac app

The Finder demo highlights the List View control without using OpenAI, so you can test the highlight first.

Guide this tab

For websites, use the Wander extension in Chrome. Open any website, click the black Wander button on the right, and ask your question there.
